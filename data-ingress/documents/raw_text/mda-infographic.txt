Amendments to 
Misuse of Drugs Act

The Misuse of Drugs (Amendment) Bill will

A. Introduce a new legislative framework for 
psychoactive substances.

B. Increase the punishment for possession of larger 
quantities of more dangerous and harmful controlled 
drugs.

A. New Legislative Framework for 
Psychoactive Substances

New Psychoactive Substances (NPS) mimic the effects of 
controlled drugs such as cannabis and heroin.

A. New Legislative Framework for 
Psychoactive Substances

To strengthen deterrence against psychoactive substances, 
the amendments will:

Introduce a new framework to control 
substances based on their capacity to produce 
a psychoactive effect, instead of their 
chemical structure, unless excluded in a 
specified Schedule.

Criminalise the trafficking, manufacturing, 
importing, exporting, possession and 
consumption of psychoactive substances, and 
allow for the arrest and detention of abusers 
for treatment and rehabilitation.

B.  Increased punishment for 
possession of larger quantities of 
controlled drugs deemed to be more 
dangerous and harmful

The current sentencing framework for drug possession 
does not sufficiently account for the potential harm that 
could be caused by persons with large amounts of 
controlled drugs in their possession.

A more deterrent punishment is necessary to deter 
the proliferation of drugs locally. 

Increased punishments, including caning, will be 
introduced for the possession of selected controlled 
drugs above certain weight thresholds.
