I’ve always loved reading.
Some stories I’ve read showed
the harms that drug abuse
can cause on a person and
their loved ones. This inspired
me to sign up as an ADA.

International Day
Against Drug Abuse

On this day, the city

Meeting new people is my favourite part! It’s
a fulfilling experience to share with others
the importance of leading a drug-free life.
