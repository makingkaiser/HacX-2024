This drug prevention poster employs a visually striking "criminal dossier" theme, featuring a bold red and yellow color palette. At the top, a grainy, muted background photo of a road sets a somber tone, juxtaposed with a vibrant red "Suspect ID No 7100: Cannabis" label, reminiscent of a police file. The centerpiece of the poster is a textual layout mimicking an investigative report, divided into sections like "Profile of the Suspect," "Criminal Fact Sheet," and urgent side notes, all aiming to detail the risks associated with cannabis use. The design uses typographic hierarchy and iconography to guide the viewer's eye, enhancing engagement and readability. A prominent, cautionary message "LIFE DOES NOT REWIND. SAY 'NO' TO DRUGS." occupies the lower portion, underscoring the irreversible consequences of drug abuse. This creative yet stark presentation leverages contrast and urgency to capture attention and convey a critical message.
