The image presents a colorful, cartoonish representation aimed at drug prevention, specifically highlighting the different classifications of New Psychoactive Substances (NPS). It features anthropomorphized characters, each symbolizing a drug category such as opioids, hallucinogens, stimulants, sedatives/hypnotics, dissociatives, and synthetic cannabinoids. This approach combines an artistic style with an educational message, making the topic accessible and less intimidating.
